Ivan Rinaldi is a Ph.D. Student at the Computational Intelligence Laboratory (CILab), University of Bari Aldo Moro. His research focuses on **Multimodal Artificial Intelligence and generative models**, with particular attention to learning relationships across different forms of information.

His work investigates **audio-visual and vision-language learning**, including methods that connect images, video, language and sound within shared or coordinated representations.
