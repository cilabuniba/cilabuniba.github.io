About CILab

CILab is a research laboratory of the Department of Computer Science at the University of Bari Aldo Moro, coordinated by Prof. Giovanna Castellano.

Our work spans intelligent data analysis and visual understanding, with particular attention to efficient and explainable methods that can move beyond benchmarks and operate in complex application domains.
