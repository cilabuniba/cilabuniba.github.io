VISTA: drone and AI for roadside rescue

The first demonstration video presents CILab research on drone-based visual assistance and AI for post-crash emergency response.

04 August 2026

The first demonstration video presents VISTA — Visual Assistance from Drones for Roadside Rescue, a CILab project developed with institutional partners to investigate how unmanned aerial systems, computer vision and artificial intelligence can improve situational awareness in the post-crash phase.
