Davide Cazzorla is a Ph.D. Student at the Computational Intelligence Laboratory (CILab), University of Bari Aldo Moro. His research focuses on Explainable Artificial Intelligence and uncertainty-aware machine learning.

His work investigates probabilistic and fuzzy approaches for representing uncertainty, including fuzzy clustering and interpretable decision-support models.

These methods are also studied in the context of AI-supported procurement and decision-making, where transparency, uncertainty and the explicit representation of evaluation criteria play a central role.
