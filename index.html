About CILab

CILab is a research laboratory of the Department of Computer Science at the University of Bari Aldo Moro, coordinated by Prof. Giovanna Castellano.

Our work spans intelligent data analysis and visual understanding, with particular attention to efficient and explainable methods that can move beyond benchmarks and operate in complex application domains.

Research in practice

Explore projects
Active

VISTA — Visual Assistance from Drones for Roadside Rescue

Research and validation of drone-based visual assistance, computer vision and AI to improve situational awareness and support post-crash emergency response.

Completed

NEED — Next-Gen Digital Diagnostics

Research on explainable and trustworthy AI methods for digital diagnostics, with a focus on multimodal medical data and clinically meaningful explanations.

Completed

FAIR — Future Artificial Intelligence Research, Spoke 6

Research within Spoke 6 — Symbiotic AI, with CILab leading WP6.4 on the understandability of AI systems and contributing methods for interpretable and human-understandable AI.
